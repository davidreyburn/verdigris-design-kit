## 02 · Territory bake-off

**Cybernetic Minimalism** — fit 1/5
> steal: hard to read, I feel nothing

**Academic Cyberpunk** — fit 3/5
> steal: very readable option, great for an article, case study or long reading. looks a little too Anthropic-coded

**Prismatic Instrument** — fit 3/5
> steal: useful in concepts that are "spectrumatic" or the 4-pillar model set by the report. strong colors anchor to multiple concepts being kept alive in the piece. in overuse it looks a little bit like slop, needs to have tight semantic connections

**Black Box** — fit 2/5
> steal: OK I personally love this but this is where it starts freaking out the non-tech literate among us and reads a bit "basement dweller". elements here would be most appropriate for small print, technical details, etc

**Signal Lab** — fit 5/5
> steal: Love this. combines readability with a tech edge that still feels like me. Especially good for "lab report" style content

**Verdigris Native** — fit —/5
> steal: better than "Academic Cyberpunk" but feels a little bit underwhemling on first blush. maybe best as long form, non-technical text approaches

**Synthesis — which is the spine?**

→ Signal Lab

**Which territories are guests, and what exactly do they contribute?**

- Verdigris Native
- Black Box
- Prismatic Instrument

**The one move you want from each guest.**

> signal lab- good for "lab report" style tones, want the graph readouts and tech style
> black box - useful for minimal, out of the way content or technical details that the general public doesn't care to see
> verdigris native= best for stuff I really want people to read and feel comfortable while they do so
> prismatic- may be useful for "spectrum" topics or multi-pillar topics

## 11 · Anti-goals

**Forbidden. Check everything that is out.**

- Glassmorphism — blur, translucent panels
- Glow and neon bloom on text or borders
- Saturated neon greens — #00FF88 and relatives
- Blinking terminal cursor in the logo
- Space Grotesk + Inter
- Gradient-filled text
- Emoji in UI or headings
- Animated gradient borders
- Stock imagery and generic 3D renders
- Parallax and scroll-jacking
- Matrix rain, falling glyphs, fake terminals typing themselves
- Dark-violet SaaS gradient backgrounds
- Skeuomorphic hardware chrome — knobs, bezels, LEDs
- Marketing testimonial carousels

**The single thing that would most embarrass you if it shipped.**

> anything that looks like a quick LLM output I didn't author

**Inverse — what must survive no matter what the system says?**

> this needs to speak to someone with an abiding love of computers, Japan, cyberpunk, technicality and love of knowledge itself. somewhere in the middle of that is the frequency of my soul

**Sites, tools, or objects whose visual language you admire — and the one thing you would take from each.**

> the e.g. suggestions were really good
