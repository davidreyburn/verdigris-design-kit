Work 04 Systems 2020 Since

Four systems

Each of these is running, not proposed. Where an employer owns the work, the case study is sanitized and says so.

A governed multi-agent organization on infrastructure I own. Four agents with explicit context boundaries, a claims layer carrying provenance, and continuity architecture built on the rule that files are truth. An outside CTO reviewed the architecture and adopted its principles into his own system. Source-grounded microlearning where an agent gathers sources, generates validated content, commits, and deploys. The corpus is the professor; the model is the tutor. Validation gates sit between generation and the deploy lane. A knowledge-backed assistant performing in a regulated domain, plus the prototyping method and community of practice built around it. Implementation detail is withheld under employer IP boundaries and the boundary itself is part of the design. A trading card game with a zero-dependency functional rules engine, AIs that consume only redacted views so they cannot cheat, and an evaluation harness most product teams would envy: property-based fuzzing, exhaustive effect coverage, deterministic replay, and golden transcripts. Identical behaviour in paper, terminal, and browser.

About AAA Contrast 2019 Since

The operator-designer

I am a product designer and creative technologist working on human-AI systems: agent experience, knowledge-backed workflows, design systems, and accessibility. I do not only design AI interfaces. I build and operate multi-agent systems, and I bring what I learn into enterprise practice.

The classical half of the work is Fortune-100 design systems, WCAG 2.2 rigour, and enough implementation fluency to argue with an engineer in good faith. The other half runs on my own infrastructure, where the consequences of a bad architecture decision land on me.

An interface that hides its own decision boundaries has not removed them. It has moved them somewhere you cannot argue with.
