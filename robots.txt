# Two different tools for two different jobs, and they do not compose.
#
# Disallow stops the fetch. Use it for bulk that should not be crawled at
# all: the instruments and the reject pile stay reachable by hand — being
# inspectable is the point of publishing them — but they are large,
# historical, and should not compete with the work in a search result.
Disallow: /calibration/
Disallow: /archive/

# noindex keeps a page OUT of the index, and it is carried in the page
# head, not here. template.html, reader.html, preview-mobile.html and
# tools/og.html use it, which is why none of them appear above: a URL
# blocked by Disallow is never fetched, so its noindex is never read and
# the URL can still be indexed from an inbound link. Blocking a page you
# want deindexed is the one combination that does not work.
